Trip overview

Two kinds of route on this map

Planned
The grey dotted line is the plan as written: chosen stops joined in order, straight point-to-point. It shows the intent of the day but ignores roads, so its distance is a rough estimate.
Live
The thick coloured line is the real drivable route, calculated on load by a routing engine that follows actual OpenStreetMap roads. Its km and drive time are measured, not estimated.
If a live route ever looks like a huge, nonsensical detour, this dashboard has already checked it against the straight-line distance and fallen back to the planned line rather than show you something wrong — see Day 2 below for why that check matters even on a well-mapped route like this one.
FieldDetail
ShapeLoop out of San Francisco. Day 1 goes south on coastal Highway 1; Day 3 returns on inland Highway 101 — a different road, not a repeat. The one honest exception is Day 2: Big Sur has no through road, so that leg is a real out-and-back on Highway 1, called out below rather than hidden.
Driving≈450 km total over 3 driving days, plus a full rest/city day in San Francisco with no driving.
Surface100% paved. No 4x4 or off-road sections — this demo intentionally shows the "easy" case.
Highway 1 through Big Sur is single-lane in places and closes for days-to-months at a time after winter storms (most recently significant closures in 2023–2024 near Regent's Slide and Rocky Creek). If it's closed, Day 2 has no real substitute — Big Sur is the reason for that day, not a detour — so check Caltrans QuickMap (quickmap.dot.ca.gov) before booking non-refundable Monterey nights.

Food & drink
Monterey/Carmel: fresh seafood on Fisherman's Wharf, a Carmel Valley wine-tasting detour if you have an extra afternoon. San Francisco: Ferry Building Marketplace for a browse-and-graze lunch, Mission-district taquerias, North Beach for Italian.
Pre-trip checklist
1 week out: check Caltrans QuickMap for Highway 1 status through Big Sur; confirm Monterey hotel parking.
48 hours out: check the coastal fog/weather forecast — mornings are often overcast even in summer; reconfirm restaurant reservations in Carmel if food is a priority.
Packing
Layers — coastal fog can be 15°C/60°F even when inland SF is warm. A light rain shell, walking shoes for the McWay Falls trail, sunglasses for the 101 return leg.
Safety, weather & fallbacks
If Highway 1 is closed south of Monterey, Big Sur is not reachable by any reasonable detour that day — the nearest alternative is to substitute a Carmel Valley wine day and skip McWay Falls, rather than force the drive. Nearest hospital to Monterey: Community Hospital of the Monterey Peninsula.
