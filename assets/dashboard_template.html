Trip overview

Two kinds of route on this map

Planned
The grey dotted line is the plan as written: chosen stops joined in order, straight point-to-point. It shows the intent of the day — which places, in which sequence — but it ignores roads, so its distance is a rough estimate.
Live
The thick coloured line is the real drivable route, calculated on load by a routing engine that follows actual OpenStreetMap roads. Its km and drive time are measured, not estimated — this is the number to trust.
One exception: brand-new or resurfaced roads aren't always in the routing engine's map data yet. If it can't find a real road for a leg, this dashboard detects the resulting huge detour (checked against the straight-line distance) and falls back to the planned line for that day rather than showing a wrong route. Trust the written plan over a live figure that looks absurd.
